# 12 — Executive Recommendations

1. **P0 — KYC Pre-Submission Validation Checker (`REC-001`):** Addresses the post-April 2026 surge in KYC rework (`PC-001`) by catching missing identity documents and v2.4 checklist omissions at point of entry.
2. **P1 — Proactive Outreach After First Early SLA Breach (`REC-002`):** Targets early-tenure (<90d) clients experiencing an initial SLA miss, evaluated via a tier-stratified randomized holdout.
3. **P1 — Capacity-Based Service Case Routing (`REC-003`):** Relieves `T-03` overload (`PC-002`) by overflowing Low/Medium cases to regional backup pools when team utilization exceeds 90%.
4. **P2 — Automated Follow-Up SLA Reminders (`REC-004`):** Reduces overdue follow-ups and repeat client contacts in capacity-constrained teams.
